# Week 1 (Nov 22–28, 2026)

The project established its initial product direction and documentation baseline: Meridian is an independent, local-first weather engine, with Solstice as its first consumer rather than a dependency. Cross-platform goals, privacy constraints, AI-assisted development rules, and clean-room research expectations are documented in the repository.

The M0.1 platform proof has produced substantial CI and dependency evidence across Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux candidate paths. The current evidence and production-stack recommendation are recorded in an open pull request; they are still subject to owner review. This is engineering/platform evidence, not meteorological forecast validation or approval to begin production engine work.

The product boundary between meteorological intelligence and app-owned lifestyle recommendations has also been clarified in a documentation pull request. The production stack and the remaining M0.1 acceptance items are not yet presented as finalized.
