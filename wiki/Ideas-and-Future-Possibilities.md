# Ideas & Future Possibilities

This page is a parking area for possibilities worth investigating. Entries here are not commitments, approved specifications, or implementation permission.

Potential research areas already reflected in the project's long-term direction include:

- regional forecast evaluation and carefully validated local adaptation;
- improved characterization of model disagreement and uncertainty;
- historical forecast verification and calibration tooling;
- radar/satellite data and nowcasting research;
- additional platform and language bindings for independent consumers.

Any idea that advances must first be checked against Meridian's product boundaries, privacy and licensing requirements, scientific evidence needs, and the authoritative roadmap. Do not use this page to bypass milestone gates.

See [PLAN.md](https://github.com/INFORMATION89/Meridian-Engine/blob/main/PLAN.md) for approved scope.
